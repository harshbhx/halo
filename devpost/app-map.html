Face Whisper app map

A phone web app for people with face blindness. You add people who agreed, from a few photos and a short note. When the camera sees one of them, it whispers who they are. Everything runs and stays in the browser on the device.

The main pieces. A face reader (face-api models in the browser) turns faces into 128-number fingerprints. A matcher compares fingerprints and waits for a steady match. A vault keeps people encrypted in the browser's database. A whisper writer turns notes into one line, and the browser's own voice speaks it.

Reference route (3 stops)

  1. src/screens/WhisperMode.tsx, anchor const tick = async: each frame is read with detectAll, every face goes through bestMatch, and the fingerprints are dropped right after; only boxes and match results are kept.
  2. src/lib/matcher.ts, anchors export function bestMatch and export class Tracker: a face counts as someone only under the threshold, clearly closer to them than to anyone else, and steady in 3 of the last 4 frames. The tracker also enforces the repeat interval.
  3. src/lib/voice.ts, anchor export async function speak: the browser voice says the whisper at the chosen volume and speed, after an optional soft chime.

To change this, start here

  • How sure before whispering: THRESHOLDS in src/lib/matcher.ts; the Tracker options in src/screens/WhisperMode.tsx (need: 3, window: 4).
  • How notes become whispers: PRONOUNS and makeWhisper in src/lib/whisper.ts; tests in tests/unit/whisper.test.ts.
  • Colours, type and spacing: the :root tokens at the top of src/styles/app.css.
  • Defaults (volume, repeat interval, strictness): DEFAULT_SETTINGS in src/lib/settings.ts.
  • Sample people: SAMPLES in src/lib/demo.ts; faces and credits in public/demo/faces/.

A practice to reuse

Measure before you trust a threshold. The spec named one uncertainty: would the 0.45 match threshold separate faces under a moving camera? Instead of guessing, the end-to-end test tests/e2e/demo-mode.spec.ts logs the real distances: the same person 0.08 to 0.33, a stranger 0.78 to 0.82. The checklist's Revisions record the result. Any project with a cut-off (a similarity score, a confidence level, a timeout) can do the same: write the open question in the plan, make a test print the real numbers, then choose the value with a visible margin.
