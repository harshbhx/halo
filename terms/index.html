Terms, in plain words

%BRAND_NAME% is a free, open-source prototype. Using it means agreeing to these few points.

What %BRAND_NAME% is not

  • Not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose, treat or measure face blindness or anything else. If you have questions about your health, talk to a doctor.
  • Not an identity check. Never use it for security, safety, access, payment or legal decisions, or to decide who to trust or let in. It can name the wrong person and it can stay silent for someone you know.
  • Not a way to identify strangers. It only knows people who agreed to be added, and it can't look anyone up.

Your responsibilities

  • Consent. You're responsible for asking each person before you add them, for adding children only with a parent's or carer's agreement, and for removing anyone the moment they ask. %BRAND_NAME% records that you asked; it can't check.
  • The law where you are. Taking photos of people and keeping notes about them may be covered by privacy or data-protection law where you live. Use %BRAND_NAME% for your own personal use, with people who know.
  • Your phone. Your people are stored on your phone. Keep it locked, use %BRAND_NAME%'s own lock if others use your phone, and keep your backup passphrase safe: nobody can recover it for you.
  • Out loud. Without earbuds your phone speaks whispers aloud. The earbud check and quiet hours help; you decide where to use it.

Optional Enhanced mode

If you turn on Enhanced mode, short text is sent to %BRAND_NAME%'s server, which uses Cloudflare Workers AI. Use of those models is subject to Cloudflare's terms. What is sent is listed on the privacy page and in the app.

No warranty
