## Roadmap

From [`scope.md`](../scope.md) and [`docs/accuracy.md`](accuracy.md); none of this is built yet.

- A real-world test with consenting adults, published honestly.
- A move to a maintained face engine (the current library is archived).
- Per-person thresholds measured at enrolment, and a yearly photo refresh.
- Smart glasses or bone-conduction audio, the form factor people asked for (pocket mode is the phone's stand-in).
- An automatic "last seen" and search by cue or place.

## FAQ

**Who is it for?** People with face blindness who can see but can't place faces, and the people who help them. See [Who it's for](../README.md#who-its-for).

**What leaves the phone?** By default nothing about faces or people: no photos, no face fingerprints, no names. The optional Enhanced mode sends only short text the sheet lists before you turn it on, and records every request in an on-phone ledger. Helper cards and consent links carry text only, in the part of the link after `#`. See the [privacy model](../README.md#privacy-by-design).

**Is it a medical device?** No. It is a prototype and a hint, not proof. It can name the wrong person and it stays silent for many people who are there; never use it to decide who to trust or let in. See the [honest limits](../README.md#honest-limits).

**Does it work offline?** Yes, after the first visit.

**Can it identify strangers?** No. It only compares faces with people who agreed to be added, and drops every other face after the comparison.
